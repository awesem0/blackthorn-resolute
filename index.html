Questions

Straight answers.

How much does mediation cost, and when do we pay?

Your initial 30-minute consultation is free. Standard sessions are R950 per hour in total, which is R475 per hour per party when split 50/50, with no VAT charged. The first hour of mediation is payable on or before the session booking date.

Is a mediated agreement legally binding in South Africa?

We record agreed terms in a structured Memorandum of Understanding or parenting plan. Once signed as a settlement agreement, made an order of court, or registered with the Family Advocate through your attorney, it is binding and enforceable.

What if the other person refuses to cooperate?

You do not need to be on good terms to mediate. We can hold separate confidential sessions, or shuttle between online breakout rooms, so you never have to argue face to face. Where litigation is pending under Rule 41A, an unreasonable refusal to mediate can carry adverse cost consequences.

Do you issue Rule 41A Mediator's Reports?

Yes. We provide the Pre-Mediation Initial Joint Minute before we begin, and issue the Outcome Joint Minute and the Form J128 / MED-3 Mediator's Report within 5 business days of completion, for filing with the Registrar.
