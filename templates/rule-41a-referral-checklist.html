2. Mandatory Statutory & Protocol Steps Checklist

  • [ ] Step 1 — Amplified Rule 41A Notice Served: Form 27 Notice delivered with summons/notice of motion or prior to applying for a civil trial date, setting out concrete reasons why the dispute is capable of mediation (or narrowing of issues).
  • [ ] Step 2 — Mediator Appointment & Conflict Check (24-Hour Turnaround): Confirm availability with Stavros Gazis (Blackthorn Resolute) at support@blackthornresolute.co.za or +27 64 898 7503.
  • [ ] Step 3 — Pre-Mediation Initial Joint Minute Signed (Rule 41A(4)(a)): Parties execute the Initial Joint Minute recording appointment of mediator, confidentiality, fee apportionment (standard 50/50 split of R950/hr total · No VAT), and time period (completed within 30 days under the Gauteng Mediation Protocol).
  • [ ] Step 4 — Suspension of Time Limits Noted (Rule 41A(4)(d)): Time limits for delivery of pleadings/steps suspended from date of signature of Initial Joint Minute until conclusion of mediation.
  • [ ] Step 5 — Concise Case Summaries Exchanged (48 Hours Prior): Each party uploads or emails a 2–3 page Without Prejudice summary + core schedules via the iMedi8 portal or email.
  • [ ] Step 6 — Outcome Joint Minute & Formal Mediator's Report (Form J128 / MED-3): Issued within 5 business days of termination of mediation confirming full settlement, partial settlement (narrowing of issues for trial), or impasse.
