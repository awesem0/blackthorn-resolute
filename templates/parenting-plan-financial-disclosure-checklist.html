Divorce, Parenting Plan & Financial Disclosure Preparation Checklist

Practical preparation guide for clients & referring family attorneys (Children's Act 38 of 2005 s33/34 & Rule 43 / Rule 58).

1. Parenting Plan & Co-Parenting Checklist (If Minor Children Are Involved)

  • [ ] Primary Residence & Weekly Routine: School-term living schedule, mid-week contact, and weekend handovers (times, locations, and transport).
  • [ ] School Holidays & Special Days: Division of April/June/September/December holidays, birthdays (children's and parents'), Mother's Day, Father's Day, and religious holidays.
  • [ ] Decision-Making & Guardianship: Joint consultation protocol on schooling, major medical/dental treatment, extracurricular activities, and relocation/travel consent.
  • [ ] Child Maintenance & Direct Expenses: Monthly cash contribution plus exact apportionment (%) of school fees, uniforms, medical aid gap cover, extra-mural costs, and tertiary education.
  • [ ] Voice of the Child & Future Dispute Clause: How age-appropriate child feedback is incorporated (Children's Act s10 & s33) and commitment to return to mediation before approaching court for future variations.
