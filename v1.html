Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on mediation fees, Rule 41A compliance, and what happens if one party is difficult.

How much does mediation cost, and when do we pay?

Your initial 30-minute consultation is 100% free. Standard mediation sessions are R950 per hour total—which is R475 per hour per party when split 50/50 between two parties, with no VAT charged. The first hour of mediation is payable on or before the session booking date.

Is a mediated agreement legally binding in South Africa?

During mediation, we record your agreed terms in a structured Memorandum of Understanding / Parenting Plan. Once signed as a Settlement Agreement (or made an Order of Court / registered with the Family Advocate via your attorney), it becomes fully binding and enforceable.

What if my ex-partner or business partner refuses to cooperate?

You do not need to be on good terms to mediate. We can hold separate confidential caucus sessions (or shuttle between Zoom breakout rooms) so you never have to argue face-to-face. If litigation is pending under Uniform Rule 41A, an unreasonable refusal to mediate can carry adverse cost consequences in court.

Do you issue Gauteng High Court Rule 41A Mediator's Reports?

Yes. We provide the Rule 41A(4)(a) Pre-Mediation Initial Joint Minute prior to commencement and issue the formal Outcome Joint Minute and Form J128 / MED-3 Mediator's Report within 5 business days of completion for filing with the Registrar.

Why Fighting Over the Air Fryer Ends Up Costing R40,000

When a marriage or long-term partnership breaks down, the arguments that burn through tens of thousands of Rands in legal fees are almost never about the law. They are about hurt, pride, and the feeling that the other person is “getting away with something.”

Once two opposing litigation attorneys start exchanging formal letters at R3,500+ an hour each over household appliances, weekend pickup times, or who keeps the dog, a R2,500 kitchen appliance quickly generates R40,000 in combined legal correspondence—money that comes directly out of your children's school fees or your post-divorce deposit.

The Mediation Alternative: In mediation, we separate emotional score-settling from the financial and parenting decisions that actually matter—settling the entire schedule in a few calm hours at R475 per party per hour.
